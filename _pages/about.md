Hao Wang is an undergraduate student at Dalian Maritime University, where he studies Computer Science and Technology and works on research problems in computer vision and multimodal learning.

His current interests include deep learning, neural network design, and visual understanding under challenging conditions. In particular, he focuses on:

- Camouflaged object detection and salient object detection
- Visual multimodal large models and multimodal representation learning
- Weakly supervised learning for remote sensing change detection

## Research

Hao Wang's recent work studies how to build robust visual models when objects are hard to separate from background clutter or when multimodal inputs are only partially available. His projects explore multimodal prompt learning, modality-agnostic model design, and cross-modal camouflaged object detection.
